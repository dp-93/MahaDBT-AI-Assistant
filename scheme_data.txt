Scheme: Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulkh Shishyavrutti Yojna
Department: Higher and Technical Education, Government of Maharashtra.
Objective: To provide financial assistance to students belonging to economically weaker sections for pursuing professional degree/diploma courses.
Eligibility - Income: Annual family income must be less than or equal to Rs. 8 Lakhs.
Eligibility - Domicile: The applicant must be a domicile of Maharashtra state.
Benefits: 50% of tuition fees and 50% of exam fees are reimbursed to the student's bank account.

Scheme: Dr. Punjabrao Deshmukh Vasatigruh Nirvah Bhatta Yojna
Department: Higher and Technical Education, Government of Maharashtra.
Objective: To provide maintenance allowance for students studying in professional courses who reside in hostels.
Eligibility - Income: Annual family income must be up to Rs. 8 Lakhs.
Eligibility - Domicile: Must be a resident of Maharashtra.
Benefits: For students in MMRDA/PMRDA/Aurangabad/Nagpur regions, an allowance of Rs. 30,000 for 10 months is provided. For other regions, it is Rs. 20,000 for 10 months.

Scheme: State Government Open Merit Scholarship
Department: Higher and Technical Education, Government of Maharashtra.
Objective: To encourage meritorious students to pursue higher education.
Eligibility - Academic: Must have secured 60% or more marks in the HSC (12th standard) examination.
Eligibility - General: Must be enrolled in a full-time graduation course. There is no income limit for this specific merit scholarship.
Benefits: A scholarship amount of Rs. 1000 per annum is awarded to the selected students.
